whatItIs: >-
  A clinical site insights platform I own at work. It brings in clinical site
  information from Veeva and shows study teams where problems are starting, at
  the global, study, country, or site level.
problem: >-
  Study teams need to know which sites are running into trouble and why. That
  information sits in Veeva, spread across millions of clinical notes, and no
  team can read them all.
howItWorks:
  - >-
    A dashboard shows trends in study metrics such as enrollment and retention
    for the study, country, or site a user selects. The team can see where a
    problem is starting and address it.
  - >-
    The platform flags new challenges as they appear and tracks issues that keep
    coming back. For each one it recommends next steps, based on what worked at
    other sites that had a similar issue.
  - >-
    A chat panel sits next to the dashboard, so a user can ask further questions
    of the data. Every answer cites its sources. Users can send a report from
    the app to Outlook to share it with the clinical study team.
